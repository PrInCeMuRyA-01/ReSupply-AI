How is this calculated?

Daily average = recorded units sold ÷ calendar days in the window. We use up to 7 completed days, starting from the first recorded sale. Days without records count as zero; missing sales will change this estimate.

If your first sale is today, we show a partial-day preview labelled “Today's estimate”. Once earlier sales exist, today's unfinished day is excluded from the average.

Average mode uses its sales-rate target. A reviewed actual forecast uses today's remaining demand plus future delivery, coverage and buffer days. Dated incoming goods reduce later shortages; late arrival does not erase an earlier shortage. Suggested buy rounds the largest projected stock gap up to whole units. Supplier delay can still cause an earlier stockout.

Selected supplier quotes supply each product's unit price and delivery time. Otherwise, we use the saved product price and default delivery time. Coverage and buffer apply to every product.

Review these quantities before buying. Planning form settings reset when the page reloads; supplier selections stay saved.

How is the budget allocated?

Out-of-stock products are considered first, followed by delivery-risk products, then other restock suggestions. Products with equal urgency follow inventory order.

Each product gets as many of its suggested units as the remaining budget covers. Any leftover budget can cover later, cheaper items. This simple priority rule may leave some products partially covered or deferred.

The plan uses selected supplier prices and delivery times when available, plus coverage and buffer settings above. Items without sales history need data; zero saved unit costs need a positive supplier quote before allocation.

Products without a supplier selection can still be estimated using their saved price. Choose suppliers before order review, and confirm shipping and tax. Building a plan leaves inventory unchanged.
